01 / System architecture

Designed as a modular
mobility safety platform.

The architecture separates proven component tests, the implemented touchscreen prototype and future multi-sensor integration. The three available MPU6050 modules are planned for the front wheel, rear wheel and frame center. Handlebar and helmet zones remain virtual in this release.

IMPACT LOGIC

MPU6050 threshold testing

A single MPU6050 with Arduino UNO was used to test acceleration, tilt and impact detection logic. This is not a five-sensor field validation.

FUTURE INTEGRATION

Sensor bus and telemetry

Three MPU6050 units require address management, e.g. TCA9548A. ESP32-P4/UNO data integration is not completed.

04 / Model & evidence

Engineering transparency
is part of the design.

Range estimation uses a transparent, editable assumption rather than invented road-test results. At 20 km/h, the assumed 60 km full-charge range yields 51.0 km at 85% battery.

estimated_range_km = 60 × (battery_percent / 100) × speed_factor

speed_factor = 1.10 (≤15 km/h) · 1.00 (16–20) · 0.90 (21–25) · 0.80 (26–30)
CONFIRMED COMPONENT TEST

Display + touch

ESP32-P4 screen and touchscreen confirmed working. Arduino UNO with one MPU6050 produced sensor readings and threshold alerts.

SIMULATED PROTOTYPE

5-zone and range UI

Five impact zones, warning states, ride controls, battery percentage and range calculations are demonstration features.

NOT YET VALIDATED

Road testing

No measured battery discharge curve, real-world range benchmark, multi-zone deployment or AI model accuracy is reported.
